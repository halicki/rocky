### **FINDING: the proposed ±1.0 position guard would void its own passing case**

The 08-26 log proposed for v5: *"a CTR read must be voided, not scored as a failure,
when the page's position moves more than ±1.0 during the test window"*, and computed
that this would turn the paired test from 1–1 into **1–0**. It checked the failing
case only. Applying it to both:

| | position at strike | today | move | under proposed rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `when-to-book` (failed) | 8.9 | **10.8** | **+1.9** | VOID |
| `how-long-to-learn` (passed) | 7.8 | **8.9** | **+1.1** | **VOID** |

**The paired test would read 0–0, not 1–0 — the amendment as drafted deletes the
loop's only successful Rule 4 read.** A guard that voids every outcome is not a
guard, it is an off switch: positions on a site this small move ±1.0 routinely from
sampling alone.

**Revised proposal, still not written** (one closed instance is not a rule): the
guard should trigger on an **adverse** move large enough to plausibly explain a
*null* result — e.g. void a **failed** read when the page fell >1.5 positions, and
never void a **pass**, since a click earned while rank was *deteriorating* is
evidence in favour of the snippet, not against it. Under that form the paired test
reads **1–0 with one void**, which is what the 08-26 log was reaching for.

Today's strike registers its guard **at strike time**, in the exact form above —
the thing the 08-19/08-20 design omitted.

### Why the band matters for target selection, not just diagnosis

The paired test says something the 08-26 log did not extract: **the passing page was
at 7.8, the failing page at 8.9 and sliding to 10.8.** A snippet fix can only work on
a result people actually see. That is a selection criterion, and it is why
`family-surf-lessons-bali` (27 @ 10.8, 0 clicks) — otherwise a near-identical
candidate on impressions — was passed over in favour of a page one impression
smaller but **2.6 positions higher**.

### The defect here is NOT the 08-19/08-20 pattern

Both earlier rescues fixed an over-155-character description that Google truncated.
**This description was 144 characters — comfortably in band.** The defect is
different and had to be named before it could be fixed:

> The first **53 of its 144 characters** restated the title **verbatim**
> (*"Can you learn to surf in Bali if you can't swim well?"*), spending **37% of the
> snippet** on a sentence the user had just read on the line above.

Recording this because it means the queue's members do not share one defect, and a
CTR rescue that only ever checks character count will miss this class entirely.
