### The finding: Rule 4's entry bar is too low to support Rule 4's read bar

This is the run's substantive output, and it is a defect in the rule rather than
in any page.

Rule 4 admits pages at **≥20 impressions**. Its reads have been scored at
**≥1 click**. The two pages that produced the paired test carried 31–37
impressions per window, where that bar was already marginal; today's only
eligible target carries 26, where it is unreachable. **A rule whose entry
threshold admits samples its own success criterion cannot detect is guaranteed
to accumulate false failures** — and the loop has already booked one
(`when-to-book`, 08-26), on a page whose position was simultaneously sliding.

Recorded now, at strike time, precisely because the alternative is to discover
it on 09-12 and then argue about whether to move the goalposts — the failure
mode three playbook versions have been spent eliminating.

**Proposed for the next META run** (not written today; Rule 4 is reachable and
amendable, but this run's single action is spent on the page itself, and one
strike is not yet evidence for a threshold):

> Rule 4's entry bar becomes **impressions sufficient to test it**, not a flat
> ≥20. A ≥1-click bar over a 14-day read needs roughly **≥70 impressions per
> 28-day window** to give even a coin-flip chance at position ~10. Below that,
> the page may still be rescued — the fix costs two lines and cannot hurt — but
> the result is registered as **UNDERPOWERED** in advance and never scored.

Under that form, today's action is still correct and the 08-26 `when-to-book`
failure would have been recorded as underpowered rather than as a loss.
