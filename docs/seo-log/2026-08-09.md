### Why fitness

The one surface with no page at all. `"fit to surf"`, `"overweight"` and `"out of shape"` appear **zero times in the entire repository**. `"fitness"` appears in twelve files and **every single instance is a passing aside inside another subject** — and, tellingly, most of them are dismissals: *"almost never a fitness problem"*, *"has almost nothing to do with raw fitness"*, *"the difference almost never comes down to fitness"*. The site keeps waving the objection away in half a sentence and has never actually answered it.

Two files make it worse. `how-long-to-learn-surfing-bali` states a *"Fitness baseline. Push-up and paddle stamina matter. If you can do 15 push-ups and swim…"*, and `when-to-book-surf-lessons-canggu` tells readers to message the coach with a *"fitness baseline ('can do 15 push-ups, swim 200m')"*. Read cold by a nervous, unfit beginner, the site currently reads as though it has an entry requirement. **That is a live conversion leak, independent of search volume**, and the new post corrects it explicitly.
