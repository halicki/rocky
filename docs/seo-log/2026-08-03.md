### Cannibalisation guard

The obvious risk is competing with `/batu-bolong-surf/` — the site's best page, 16 clicks and 1470 impressions at position 9.2, which owns the brand-nav and surf-report terms. The post is built specifically not to:

- **Title, H1 and every H2 avoid** "surf report", "forecast" and "conditions". The one surf H2 is framed as "what the wave is really like" and its job is to hand off.
- **Surf intent is routed outward by link**, not answered: `/batu-bolong-surf`, `/blog/batu-bolong-surf-forecast`, `/blog/is-batu-bolong-good-for-beginners` all appear as explicit exits.
- **The intents are genuinely disjoint** — getting there, parking, swimming safety, rip currents, time of day, sunset, the temple, etiquette. None of it exists on the commercial page.

This is the same hub-and-spoke shape the loop already runs successfully across three Batu Bolong pages.
