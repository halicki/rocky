note ("232 image impressions at pos 80.8") is therefore **stale**: image search has gone from ~236
impressions to **zero**. That kills Rule 5's *first* condition (≥100 image impr at pos >50) and is
exactly why the OR-branch exists.

on every `<Image>` (audited today — 2 components, 0 missing `alt`), and both `ImageObject`/
`ImageGallery` JSON-LD and the `<image:image>` sitemap section shipped on **2026-04-30**. The
filename audit was the only one left, and it was the one the trigger pointed at.
