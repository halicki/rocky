### Why this page is not blocked by the open canonical read, decided on the registration text

`/blog/what-to-expect-first-surf-lesson-bali` is one of the **three named arms** of the 08-31
canonical sweep, whose first read is **09-14** — six days away. Orthogonality blocks
re-targeting a page with a pending action, so the question had to be answered before acting,
and it was answered from the **registration** rather than from convenience.

The 08-31 log registers its prediction as **INDEX-FORM CONVERGENCE**: *"the three slash-less
entries above should disappear from the GSC page report and reappear in the slash form."* The
metric is **which URL form Google indexes** — not the page's impression volume, not its
position, not its CTR. A JSON-LD block added to the document body cannot influence URL
canonicalisation by any mechanism. The read is immune, in the same way and for the same
*class* of reason that 09-07 established the sweep could not contaminate Read 8 — a dated,
mechanical argument, not a judgement call.

Stated as a limit rather than buried: had the registration been written on impressions, this
action would have been declined and Rule 6 would have descended to priority #3.
