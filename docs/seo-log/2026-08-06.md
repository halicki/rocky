### Cannibalisation guard (08-03 pattern, applied in reverse)

The 08-03 guard kept a new post off an existing page's intent. Here the risk runs the other way — a Canggu school writing about Kuta could easily produce a doorway page. Guards applied:

- **The post is genuinely about the south strip**, not a Canggu pitch wearing a Kuta title. Kuta is described as *"one of the best beginner waves in Indonesia"* — which is true, and which a doorway page would never concede.
- **"Near me" appears nowhere** in title, H1 or any H2 — that phrasing belongs to `/blog/surf-lessons-near-me-canggu/`.
- **No "vs" framing** in the title or H1, so it does not compete with `canggu-vs-uluwatu` or `private-vs-group` for comparison-shaped queries. The Canggu comparison is confined to **one** of nine H2s and is explicitly two-sided ("Stay south if… / Come north if…").
- **No forecast, MSW or Surfline language anywhere**, keeping clear of the locked `/blog/canggu-surf-forecast/` read.
- **Price detail stays summary-level** and hands the full breakdown off by link rather than re-deriving `/surf-lesson-prices-bali/`.

House editorial honesty is the guard that does the real work: the post tells a reader with one free afternoon in Kuta **not** to make the drive, and closes by crediting Kuta with teaching most of Bali's surfers — including the coaches now working in Canggu.
