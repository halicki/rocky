## Topic selection — why swimming ability

The 07-29 log left two standing directives: do **not** write a sixth technique post, and consider extending the **demographic/objection-reassurance** cluster, since that cluster just produced the site's newest converter (`learn-to-surf-over-40-bali`, 0→2 clicks at 18.2% CTR after five weeks at zero).

That is both the accurate answer and, on the evidence of this cluster, the converting one.
